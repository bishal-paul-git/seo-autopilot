# SEO Autopilot — Product Discovery Questions & Owner Answers
Date: 2026-11-08. Purpose: durable reminder for owner, Claude, reviewers and future sessions.
These are **decision records**, not implementation evidence. Questions are normalized/paraphrased from progressive planning discussions; answers preserve the owner's decisions. Later corrections supersede earlier choices.

| # | Question / decision sought | Owner answer / confirmed decision |
|---|---|---|

## Superseded answers — do not implement
- Earlier: "collaborative client portal with agency administrative control" was interpreted as clients talking to SEO God. **Superseded**: reporting + advisory submissions only.
- Earlier: approval authority configurable per client (agency/client/both). **Superseded**: agency-only approvals.
- Earlier assistant asked whether clients should see or talk to SEO God; later owner explicitly prohibited it. Do not reopen as an implicit default.

## Clarifications and implementation interpretation
- "Autonomous" is constrained by an approved strategy and explicit permissions, and never overrides independent review or high-risk approval.
- Client submissions are untrusted reference material; SEO God may analyze and recommend but cannot autonomously implement their contents without agency adoption.
- Eight departments and specialist roster describe the TARGET product; current repository agent definitions do not prove unattended execution.
- Agency-ready is the first production milestone; self-service SaaS is later.
- Questions not yet answered should be recorded as OPEN, not guessed: exact paid provider choices, CMS adapter priorities, initial pilot websites, formal SLA, retention periods, precise risk thresholds, agency team roles and pricing.
