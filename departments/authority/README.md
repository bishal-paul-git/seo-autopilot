# Authority (off-page and backlinks)

Earns links and mentions honestly and makes the business a clear, trusted entity.

- **Reads:** Mentions, the site's best pages, the owner's real relationships and profiles.
- **Produces:** Link opportunities and outreach drafts for a person to send; entity fixes for Technical to apply.
- **Rule:** Never sends outreach and never buys or exchanges links that pass ranking signals.
