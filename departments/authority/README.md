# Authority (off-page and backlinks)

Earns links and mentions honestly and makes the business a clear, trusted entity.

- **Reads:** Inbound-link exports (`inbound` job), mentions, the site's best pages, the owner's real relationships and profiles.
- **Produces:** Link opportunities and outreach drafts for a person to send; entity fixes for Technical to apply.
- **Rule:** Never sends outreach and never buys or exchanges links that pass ranking signals.

## Agents
| Agent | Job | Runs | Can change files? |
|---|---|---|---|
| [seo-backlink-auditor](agents/seo-backlink-auditor.md) | Audits inbound links from a Search Console or tool export: links to reclaim, new and lost sites, patterns to review | on request, from `inbound.json` | no |
| [seo-entity-reputation](agents/seo-entity-reputation.md) | Makes the business a clear, consistent, trusted entity; branded search and reviews | on request | no |
| [seo-link-builder](agents/seo-link-builder.md) | Finds honest link opportunities and drafts outreach for you to send | on request | no |

## Hand-offs
Work arrives from Command (the agent plan or a person) and leaves through Quality before a person approves it. Each agent's **Output** section says exactly what it hands on and to whom. See the [org chart](../README.md).
