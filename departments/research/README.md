# Research and intelligence

Finds what is worth doing: topics, questions, competitors and gaps, from real data.

- **Reads:** Search Console queries and pages, the keyword backlog, news digest, live search results.
- **Produces:** Topic briefs and competitor gap notes for Content and Command.
- **Rule:** Never invents search volumes or competitor numbers. With no data it says so.

## Agents
| Agent | Job | Runs | Can change files? |
|---|---|---|---|
| [seo-competitor-analyst](agents/seo-competitor-analyst.md) | Studies who ranks above you and finds gaps you can fill with real material | on request | no |
| [seo-researcher](agents/seo-researcher.md) | Finds topics worth a page and writes the brief | on request, topic proposals | no |
| [seo-serp-analyst](agents/seo-serp-analyst.md) | Tracks positions, search features and share of voice on the queries that matter | on request, weekly or monthly | no |
| [seo-topic-mapper](agents/seo-topic-mapper.md) | Maps every topic to one owner page: clusters, intent, potential, gaps and overlaps | on request, before a content plan | no |

## Hand-offs
Work arrives from Command (the agent plan or a person) and leaves through Quality before a person approves it. Each agent's **Output** section says exactly what it hands on and to whom. See the [org chart](../README.md).
