# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to make websites better without tricks. We expect everyone taking part (issues, pull requests, discussions) to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- Not harass, demean or exclude anyone, and not share other people's private information.
- Not propose or contribute anything designed to deceive search engines or readers (fake reviews, fabricated data, link schemes, mass-generated spam).

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these expectations, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory on this repository (Security tab > Report a vulnerability) and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence.
